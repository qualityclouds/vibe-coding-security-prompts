# The 10-question checklist

Answer yes or no. Any "no" or "I don't know" has a prompt that helps.

| # | Question | If not, run |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do customers have to log in before they see their own data? | [Log-in and accounts](prompts/03-login-and-accounts.md) |
| 2 | If I log in as customer A, is it impossible to see customer B's orders, messages or details? | [Database and data access](prompts/02-database-and-data-access.md) |
| 3 | Is the database protected so it only answers people who are allowed to ask? | [Database and data access](prompts/02-database-and-data-access.md) |
| 4 | Are all API keys and passwords kept out of the code that runs in the browser? | [Secrets and API keys](prompts/04-secrets-and-api-keys.md) |
| 5 | Do prices, discounts and permissions get decided on the server, not in the browser? | [Client and server](prompts/01-client-and-server.md) |
| 6 | Do my forms reject nonsense, oversized or harmful input? | [Screens and forms](prompts/05-screens-and-forms.md) |
| 7 | Do I know what personal data I store, and can a customer ask me to delete theirs? | [Personal data and GDPR](prompts/06-personal-data-and-gdpr.md) |
| 8 | If I deleted my data by mistake today, could I get it back? | [Backups and recovery](prompts/08-backups-and-recovery.md) |
| 9 | Would a bot filling my forms 10,000 times cost me money or break the app? | [Cost and abuse](prompts/09-cost-and-abuse.md) |
| 10 | Has someone who knows software architecture looked at the app before payments or sensitive data go in? | [When to ask a person](README.md#when-to-ask-a-person) |
